F.A.Q.

Do I need a membership to watch reviews?

Every review is free to watch. A membership adds the full archive, workshop guides and invitations to track days. We never lock a review behind a paywall, because the point of the site is honest information that anyone can use before spending serious money on a car.

How often do you publish?

A full review goes up every Thursday, and shorter workshop pieces follow through the week. Long term tests get an update at every service interval, so you can see how a car ages rather than just how it feels on delivery day.

Can I suggest a car to review?

Yes. Members can nominate a car in the forum, and each quarter we film the three most requested. If the car is rare we will ask owners in the community whether one of them would lend it for a day in exchange for a copy of the footage.

Do you take manufacturer sponsorship?

No. Every car is bought or privately borrowed, and we say so at the start of each review. Press cars arrive prepared by the factory and tell you very little about the example you would actually buy from a dealer.
